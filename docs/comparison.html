A different center of gravity

MGA does not need other launchers to be bad.

Playnite, LaunchBox, LANCommander, and the Xbox app each solve real problems well. MGA is for a different combination: a household-accessible web interface, several devices, concrete game copies, and multiple ways to play without flattening the collection.

Store apps

Best for buying, installing, updating, and playing inside their own ecosystems. MGA sits above those connections instead of replacing them.

Playnite

Strong desktop unification, customization, themes, extensions, and fullscreen use across stores and emulators.

LaunchBox

Strong collection presentation, rich media, emulation, metadata, and living-room frontend workflows.

LANCommander

Strong self-hosted game distribution for private networks, install archives, launchers, and LAN-focused libraries.

MGA's focus

The source, play option, and target are separate choices.

QuestionHow MGA answers it
What games do I have access to?One profile library across configured stores, subscriptions, files, ROMs, shared storage, and cloud services.
Where did this version come from?Concrete copies retain their connection, platform, edition, files, and ownership—even when grouped under one game.
How can I play it?One Play menu can offer installed, storefront, browser, emulator, cloud, or remote-device routes.
Where will it run?The browser chooses a paired device and Windows user; MGA checks capability and local state there.
Will progress follow?Achievements remain provider-aware, and save compatibility is explicit rather than assumed.
Who controls the data?The local server owns the database, media, profiles, bindings, and managed install roots.

Choose MGA when

Your collection crosses boundaries.

  • You want to browse and act from a TV, phone, tablet, or another PC
  • More than one person needs isolated storefront and cloud connections
  • The same game exists in several stores, editions, regions, or emulators
  • You want local installs, browser play, and cloud play in one decision
  • You want self-hosted control without losing provenance or device ownership

Use the best tool for the job

MGA is pre-1.0. If you primarily want a polished desktop launcher today, Playnite may be the better fit. If rich collection presentation is the priority, consider LaunchBox. If packaged LAN distribution is the whole job, LANCommander is purpose-built for it.

MGA earns its place when those concerns overlap and the collection must remain understandable across people, devices, sources, and play methods.
