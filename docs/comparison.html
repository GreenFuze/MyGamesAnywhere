Pick what fits your game room

Great launchers already exist. MGA solves a different mix.

Playnite, LaunchBox, LANCommander, RomM, and store apps each do important things well. MGA is for homes that need a browser library, separate players, several gaming PCs, and a mixed modern-PC and retro collection.

Store apps

Best for buying, installing, updating, and playing inside their own ecosystems. MGA sits above those connections instead of replacing them.

Playnite

Strong desktop unification, customization, themes, extensions, and fullscreen use across stores and emulators.

LaunchBox

Strong collection presentation, rich media, emulation, metadata, and living-room frontend workflows.

LANCommander

Strong self-hosted game distribution for private networks, install archives, launchers, and LAN-focused libraries.

RomM

Strong self-hosted ROM management, browser play, handheld sync, and a polished retro-focused experience.

What MGA brings together

Find the game first. Choose how and where to play next.

QuestionHow MGA answers it
What games do I have access to?One player library across stores, subscriptions, files, ROMs, shared storage, and cloud services.
What if I own it more than once?Steam, Xbox, different editions, and regional releases can appear together without becoming indistinguishable.
How can I play it?One Play menu can offer an installed game, store launcher, configured browser or emulator route, xCloud, or another PC.
Where will it run?Pick a connected PC and MGA checks what is installed and ready there.
Will my progress follow?MGA shows which achievements and saves belong to each version, and only offers safe, compatible sync choices.
Who controls the data?Your MGA Server keeps the library, artwork, players, and device connections on hardware in your home.

Choose MGA when

Your collection crosses boundaries.

  • You want to browse and act from a TV, phone, tablet, or another PC
  • More than one person needs isolated storefront and cloud connections
  • The same game exists in several stores, editions, regions, or emulators
  • You want supported local installs, browser play, and xCloud availability in one decision
  • You want your library on your hardware instead of depending on another online account

Use the best tool for the job

MGA is pre-1.0. If you primarily want a polished desktop launcher, Playnite may be the better fit. Choose LaunchBox for rich collection presentation, RomM for a retro-first server, or LANCommander for focused LAN game distribution.

MGA earns its place when those needs overlap: several players, several devices, and a collection that has grown beyond one store.
